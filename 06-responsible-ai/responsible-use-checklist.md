# Responsible AI Use Checklist

This section applies responsible AI principles to the **Business Analyst / GovConnect** scenario used throughout this portfolio.

## Accuracy
AI-generated content in this portfolio was checked against source material before use — for example, the Information Workflow's first AI draft incorrectly marked the permit-module data refresh as "Done" and invented a deadline for the accessibility review; both were caught and corrected against the raw meeting notes. The Verification pass also caught an internal inconsistency (an outdated priority rating) between the Information Workflow and Planning Workflow sections.

## Accountability
Every deliverable in this portfolio that could be sent to a real stakeholder — the Writing Workflow email, the Information Workflow brief — carries an explicit human-approval step, and none were marked "approved" until actually reviewed and confirmed. Final responsibility for anything used from this portfolio rests with the trainee, not the AI.

## Confidentiality
GovConnect is a fictional government portal invented for this course; no real department, government system, or internal organizational data is referenced anywhere in the portfolio.

## Privacy
No real citizen or personal data appears anywhere in this portfolio. Where the scenario involves citizen-facing features (e.g., the Service Request Tracker), examples reference the feature and process only — never an actual person's data.

## Bias
Draft outputs were checked for overconfident or unsupported framing — for example, the Information Workflow's first AI draft turned an open question (license-renewal scope) into a stated decision, and assigned an owner to the accessibility review that the source never named. Both were corrected rather than left as confident-sounding guesses.

## Human Oversight
Every section that produces a stakeholder-facing or decision-relevant output (Writing Workflow, Information Workflow, Verification) includes a documented human review step, and nothing was finalized without that review.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

| Portfolio Information Item | Classification | Why | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stakeholder Delay Update Email content | Green | Generic project-communication content, already intended for distribution to the stakeholder team; no confidential specifics | Safe to use as drafted |
| UAT status details — open bugs, blockers, pending decisions | Amber | Internal project-readiness information; reveals delivery gaps that shouldn't circulate beyond the project team without review | Keep within the project/sponsor audience; do not share externally without review |
| Real citizen application data (e.g., a citizen's ID or personal permit details) | Red *(not used anywhere in this portfolio)* | Would be personally identifiable citizen data tied to a government service | Must never be entered into an AI tool or this repository; this portfolio only ever references the feature/process, never actual citizen data |

### Red Boundary
No Red information is included anywhere in this repository. Real citizen personal data, internal credentials, or actual government department records would be classified as Red and must not be entered into public AI tools or uploaded to GitHub.
