# Professional Writing Example

**Scenario:** The GovConnect Service Request Tracker was scheduled to launch on October 5. A third-party identity-verification dependency has been delayed, pushing launch to October 19. This is a confirmed decision from the project sponsor.

## 1. AI-Generated Draft

**Subject:** Launch Update

Hi team, I wanted to let you know that the launch date for the tracker has changed. We ran into some issues with a vendor and needed more time. The new date is coming soon. Let me know if you have questions.

Thanks,
[Name]

## 2. Fact Verification

| Item | Verification Result |
|---|---|
| Original launch date | Confirmed: October 5 |
| New launch date | Confirmed: October 19 |
| Reason | Confirmed as a third-party identity-verification delay |
| Impact on other milestones | Not confirmed — removed from the final version |
| Statement that new date was "coming soon" | Inaccurate — the new date is already confirmed; corrected |

## 3. Tone Refinement
Adjusted to name the audience (non-technical department stakeholders), state both exact dates, name the reason plainly without over-apologizing, and drop any claim not covered in the verification table above.

## 4. Human Review and Sign-off

I confirmed that the final version:
- includes both exact dates;
- gives the confirmed reason only;
- does not assert unconfirmed impact on other milestones;
- does not blame a vendor or team by name;
- is concise and suitable for department stakeholders.

**Human approval status:** Approved.

## 5. Final Version

**Subject:** Service Request Tracker — Launch Date Moved to October 19

Dear Team,

The Service Request Tracker launch is moving from **October 5** to **October 19**, due to a delay in a third-party identity-verification integration.

Please review your own timelines for anything that may depend on this launch date, and raise any concerns through the project channel.

Happy to answer any questions before then.

Best regards,
Shrouq Alzahrani
Business Analyst — GovConnect
